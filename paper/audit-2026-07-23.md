33. **_extremal_levy float32 corner: spurious right-tail draws.** Where
    cos(φ−α(φ−φ₀)) crosses zero, float32 absolute error (~6e-8) swamps
    the true value — occasionally flipping its sign into the 1e-12 clip —
    and the ^{−(α−1)/α} power amplifies the error into impossible
    positive draws (α=1.5: +64 in 30M where the true max is ~8; α=1.95:
    +259; the true right tail is stretched-exponential, dead by ~+10).
    At production α=1.8: 0 spurious draws in 360M across 12 seeds —
    latent, not active. **Root cause found (your "check the existing
    clipping" call): eps mismatch in the port.** scaleinvariance's
    `_extremal_levy_core` clips at a precision-scaled eps (~100× machine
    eps: 1e-6 in its float32 default) and is clean at all α in both
    precisions (verified, 30M draws each); STEAM's port kept the clip
    structure but hardcoded the float64-scale `eps = 1e-12` into the
    float32 pipeline, letting garbage cosines in the 1e-12–1e-8 range
    through the negative power. Verified fix: `eps = np.float32(1e-6)`
    in `_extremal_levy` — identical port code is then clean at 1.5/1.8/
    1.95 (max ~7, log⟨e^γ⟩ exact to 4 digits). The larger eps also caps
    the legitimate far-negative tail at smaller magnitude, which is
    invisible through exp(γ) (both map to multiplier −1). **APPLIED
    2026-07-27 (complete-the-code directive), pending your read** —
    `eps = np.float32(1e-6)` with the full mechanism in the comment.
